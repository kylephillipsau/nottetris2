Not Tetris 2

Tetris, but the pieces obey physics.

 

Best on a keyboard. Touch controls work for one player, or player 2 in versus.

  • ←→ move
  • ↓ drop
  • ZX rotate
  • Enter select / pause
  • Esc back
Versus: player 1 ADS GH · player 2 ←→↓ numpad 12
Original game by Maurice Guégan (Stabyourself.net)
